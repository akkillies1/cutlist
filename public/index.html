Designed for responsible handoff

Visual confidence is not manufacturing certification.

DCODE Low Balance helps structure design and production information. The person responsible for the job must still verify the original drawing, final dimensions, material specifications, hardware model, clearances and machining details before release.

Read the release checklist ↗
✓
Check source dimensionsExtracted values are candidates until checked against the original drawing and scale.
✓
Confirm manufacturer dataHinge, runner, sliding and machining allowances must come from the exact selected system.
✓
