THE PRACTICAL OPERATING GUIDE

From an idea to a reviewable furniture design.

A plain-language field guide for homeowners, designers, carpenters and factory reviewers. Begin visually, check the measurements, and keep the production questions visible all the way through.

Important: a preview is not a factory approval.Design dimensions, extracted drawing values, hardware assumptions, cost estimates and geometry checks still need the responsible person to compare them with the actual site, source drawing, supplier data and construction plan.

Start with a picture or your own words

Open the studio and choose Simple to begin. On the first screen, use one of two paths: select a furniture template, or describe the idea in the prompt. The built-in prompt recognises a small set of clearly stated options—such as width, height, depth, drawer count, a loft, door type or a finish. It works locally; it is not an AI design authority.

  1. Choose a starting template such as Wardrobe, Kitchen, TV unit or Vanity, or enter a short description.
  2. If you used the prompt, choose Review my idea. Read the proposed values, then choose Apply proposal & continue. Unrecognized details are not guessed.
  3. Name the project, room and furniture design in the guided setup. These names are editable and help keep saved work organized.
  4. Continue through the steps. You can return to earlier choices; the existing model and its current limits remain in effect.

Measurements in the prompt that omit a unit are treated as centimetres in the initial proposal; the editor stores and displays design dimensions in millimetres. Check every converted value against a tape measure or approved drawing before relying on it.

Choose the level of control you need

All three levels use the same underlying design. Changing the mode changes which specialist workspaces are shown; it does not remove the need to review the design.

The design journey

1 · Confirm the space and dimensions

Use measured width, height and depth; identify which face or wall the measurement refers to. Also check door swings, skirting, fillers, levels, wall obstructions, services, windows, appliance niches and room access. The app uses millimetres for its parametric fields. Never substitute a standard room size for a site measurement.

2 · Customize what belongs in the furniture

Select a relevant template and review its component proposal. Depending on the design, customize shelves, hanging bays, partitions, drawers, lofts, doors, accessories and room layout. The display is a coordination model; a part only reaches the cut list through the app’s applicable workflow and review rules.

3 · Select visible finishes

Choose a visual colour/finish for the preview and record material, board grade, brand, decor code, grain and edge-band details when they are known. A screen swatch is not a colour match or supplier specification. Keep unknown values marked for verification instead of filling them with a guess.

4 · Review warnings and unresolved details

Read each fit, geometry and manufacturing message. Resolve critical or incompatible dimensions with a measured value or exact manufacturer data. Missing prices may remain pending; missing drilling depth or clearance must not silently become a production instruction.

Inspect the 2D and 3D views together

The design editor’s 2D and 3D previews are review tools built from the design’s current inputs. Use 2D drawing-view controls for the relevant front/elevation and plan context. Switch to the 3D preview to inspect volume, orientation, openings and component placement. In the 3D view, drag to orbit, use the wheel or trackpad to zoom, and select Reset view to restore its default camera.

  1. Compare overall width, height and depth first. Check the unit and current furniture identity.
  2. Check board thickness, back style, shelf positions, partitions, loft, doors, drawers, gaps and visible accessories.
  3. Look for orientation changes, overlaps, missing pieces and unexpected openings. View the assembly from more than one angle where possible.
  4. After changing a dimension or component, wait for both projections to update and review the related cut-list row again.
  5. If the original drawing, a preview and a proposed part disagree, stop the handoff. Record the inputs and resolve the mismatch first.

The Design Studio’s 2D views are dimensioned design proposals, not necessarily the authoritative source of cut-part geometry. The assembly and panel tools have their own views and model limits. None of these previews generate a complete joinery, drilling or CNC machining plan.

Designer tools: panels, assemblies and rooms

Panel Geometry

Use Dimensions for a rectangular part, or 3-point geometry when the panel is defined by measured points. Give it a part name, working plane, thickness and offsets. Compare the face, plan and side projections with the isometric panel before adding its unapproved proposal to the cut list. Verify finished-versus-cut-size conventions, materials, grain and exposed edges.

Assembly Builder

Choose a preset and set the overall envelope and board parameters, then build the assembly. Review its object list, boundaries, opening sizes, intersections and required face contacts in front/plan/side and 3D. Board panels transfer to the board cut list; mirrors, glass, fittings and decorative placeholders use separate schedules. A contact report is a geometric check—not a verified dowel, confirmat, groove, rebate, hinge drilling or CNC operation.

Room planning and kitchens

Use available room and kitchen planning controls to sketch a layout, define runs/modules, place appliances and inspect clearance warnings. Confirm service locations, appliance manufacturer envelopes, door/aisle clearances and site dimensions against current plans. A room layout is a proposal until checked against the real room.

Factory mode and the Manufacturing Cutlist

Use the cut list as a review queue—not as an automatic release. Inspect each intended part and confirm its identity, length, width, thickness, quantity, board material/grade, surface finish, decor code, grain direction, edge-band coverage, liner/backer and relevant machining notes. Check retained manual and drawing-imported rows for duplicates before regeneration or handoff.

Changing geometry or specifications can mark generated cut-list data stale or return rows to review. Regenerate if required, inspect all preserved source rows and validate the result again. A checked row records a human review; it does not certify production.

Bring in an existing drawing

Open Drawings & Cut Lists from the main navigation when starting from an existing document. Use one of the formats offered by the current import control. Work page by page for multi-page files; inspect source labels, view context, dimensions and scale before accepting any part. Do not treat a scanned outline, OCR, AI vision or automatically detected dimension pair as a confirmed cutting part.

  1. Load the original drawing and confirm its page/view and stated units.
  2. Calibrate image scale using a known dimension where applicable; compare with original vector data if available.
  3. Review candidate dimensions and outlines; select only the part you can associate with evidence on the drawing.
  4. Complete part thickness, quantity, material and finish only when supported by a specification or an explicit human decision.
  5. Review the consolidated list, verify it against the source, and export only after checking all intended rows.

The optional provider/API fields are user-supplied settings. Never paste sensitive keys into an untrusted machine or treat a provider’s generated interpretation as authoritative. Follow the on-screen storage note for whether each key is held only in the current tab or deliberately saved in that browser profile.

Materials, finishes, hardware and cost

  • Visual colour: a preview approximation only; verify actual board and laminate samples under the expected light.
  • Decor code: use the supplier’s exact collection/product code, not only a colour name.
  • Grain and edge band: specify direction and exposed edges for each part, with thickness/colour to match the schedule.
  • Hardware rules: hinge placement, shutter size, runner envelope, drilling and track deductions depend on the exact current system and its manufacturer data.
  • Cost estimate: missing prices must remain pending. Rate registers may contain historical/provisional values; obtain a dated supplier quote before purchasing.

Save, export and recover your work

The editor stores project/design state in this browser. It does not automatically sync every draft to another device. Check the save-status message, export a project/design backup when the controls are available, and keep the original site/drawing files in the project folder outside the browser.

  1. Use a recognizable project, room and furniture name.
  2. Export a project backup before clearing browser storage, changing devices or doing substantial revision work.
  3. Keep exported CSV/SVG/JSON with the source drawing and its revision; note which values were confirmed by whom.
  4. After importing a backup, check project identity, design dimensions, components, part count and material fields. Import is intended to create a separate copy rather than silently overwrite the project.
  5. Before continuing an existing project, confirm whether its design, panel geometry, assembly and cut list are connected or are still separate local workspaces.

Troubleshooting

What you seeWhat to do next
The prompt found no choicesTry clear words and explicit dimensions (for example, “240 cm wide”). Continue manually if it does not recognize the request.
The template looks wrongReturn to the category and template, then inspect all preset dimensions and components before continuing.
3D preview is blankCheck browser WebGL and network access for the Three.js library, then use the 2D drawing view and on-screen fallback message.
2D and 3D seem differentCheck which model and revision each view represents. Stop manufacturing handoff if you cannot reconcile them.
Cut list is staleRegenerate only after confirming the intended source; review retained manual/imported parts and inspect the refreshed rows.
Part values are missingUse a source drawing, supplier datasheet or measurement; leave unsupported values unconfirmed.
A project is missing on another deviceReturn to the original browser/device or restore a project backup; local drafts do not automatically sync.
Cloud save/hardware rate is unavailableConfirm the hosted service and permissions. The local browser draft is not the same as a successful cloud save.

Before a factory handoff

  • Project, room, furniture name and drawing revision match the job.
  • All site dimensions, openings, services and obstructions are measured and checked.
  • Template components, construction, clearances and door/drawer details are reviewed.
  • 2D projections and the 3D preview are compared using the same design inputs.
  • Every intended cut-list part has a confirmed dimension, quantity, material, finish and edge treatment, or an explicit hold for verification.
  • Exact manufacturer hardware data and its reference/revision are checked where sizing or drilling depends on it.
  • The cut list is current after the last geometry/specification change; retained manual/imported rows are intentional.
  • Exports and backup are stored with the source drawing and revision.
  • The responsible designer/factory operator signs off before manufacture or CNC release.
Stop if a critical fact is unknown.A complete-looking model, zero intersections, filled review checklist or exported file is not proof that the furniture is safe, compatible or ready to manufacture.
